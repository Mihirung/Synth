How to play

  • Drag blocks from the tray onto the disc; drag one off the disc to remove it.
  • Turn a block's inner ring for its main control; slide the outer arc for the second.
  • Tap a block to change its mode (waveform, filter type, loop…). On a real cube, turn it to another face.
  • Place a Rec block to record the next bars and loop them; tap it to record again. Turn it to set the length.
  • A Mic block is live input as soon as it is on the table.
  • Tap a sequencer's pads to switch steps on; pull a pad outward for a higher note.
  • Tap the small keys button above an oscillator for its piano; draw a shape beside one to become its waveform.
  • Push two oscillators together for FM; swipe across a line to cut it. Play the A–L keys or a MIDI keyboard.
  • More opens the advanced pucks (tuning, envelopes, chance, scenes, stems…). Put one near the block it should change; the global ones work anywhere.
