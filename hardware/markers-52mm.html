Lumatable marker sheet · 52 mm

Print at 100% (no scaling) on matte white card. Cut each disc out with a 3 mm white margin, and stick it to the top of a cube or a puck (the 3D-printed ones have a shallow pocket the disc drops into). The small offset dot is the heading: turning the object turns its ring. Each object type has four faces; a cube carries four faces of one object plus two blanks, so turning it over changes its mode.

Heading dot at 0.38 D, ID dots at 0.24 D on eight 45° slots (256 ids). The second section is the advanced set: hexagonal pucks in the printed kit.
