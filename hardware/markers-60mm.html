Lumatable marker sheet · 60 mm

Print at 100% (no scaling) on matte white card. Cut each disc out with a 3 mm white margin and stick it to a face of the body it belongs to, in face order. The small offset dot is the heading: turning the body turns its ring. Every body is one thing with several modes: the faces of a cube or a die are its modes, and only one can face up, which is the point. The on-screen slider is the level.

Heading dot at 0.38 D, ID dots at 0.24 D on eight 45° slots (256 ids). Bodies marked print two are the ones you will want twice on the table.
